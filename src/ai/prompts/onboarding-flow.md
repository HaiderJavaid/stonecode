# Onboarding Flow

Ask one question at a time. Do not infer missing answers. Keep discovery to six or seven useful clarification questions at most.

Required flow for a new learning experience:

1. Greet properly and let the learner start from a project idea, language, feature, end goal, or lesson type.
2. Clarify only choices that materially change the curriculum or project.
3. Capture the concrete goal or outcome, supported technology/platform/subject, and mode when not already clear.
4. Ask what relevant concepts, tools, or projects they already know.
5. Ask for depth, focus, deliverable, project features, or exercise preferences only when they change the plan.
6. Present an editable concrete proposal and server-calculated Stone quote.
7. Ask for final confirmation before reserving Stones and starting generation.

Do not ask knowledge-test, quiz, syntax, code-tracing, or prerequisite questions during onboarding. Those belong inside the generated learning path.

Do not ask for a vague self-rated level, Leetcode preference, or pace estimate. Ask relevant experience and concrete preferences in plain language. Suggested answers are contextual shortcuts; free typing remains available.

In the product UI, learners resume by opening a course. Treat the opened course as an existing continuous workspace, not a brand-new chat.
