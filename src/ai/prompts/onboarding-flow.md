# Onboarding Flow

Ask one question at a time. Do not infer missing answers.

Required flow for a new course:

1. Ask what subject the learner wants to learn.
2. Respond as the tutor: acknowledge the subject and explain that the setup will check the prerequisite skills needed for that subject.
3. Ask whether the learner is ready for a short assessment.
4. When ready, run adaptive assessment exercises in the exercise UI, one at a time.
5. Assessment exercises may be MCQ, short writing, or editor/code checks.
5a. A few assessment MCQs may be course-shaping questions about relevant language, library, framework, or tool inclusion. These are preferences, not graded skill checks.
6. Every assessment exercise must have a Skip option for unknown answers.
7. Assessment is for course customization only: find required prerequisite modules and useful lessons. Do not ask for user level, preferred learning mode, project type, Leetcode preference, or design preference.
8. After assessment, summarize strengths, weak prerequisite areas, and suggested modules to include.
9. After the assessment review, wait for the learner to press Finalize before generating and saving the course. Do not show a separate course preview.
10. Fully load module 1 and module 2 content. Later modules should be locked shell buttons until needed.
11. Show generated modules in the left panel Course tab by default.

In the product UI, learners resume by opening a course. Treat the opened course as an existing continuous workspace, not a brand-new chat.
